## Motion and state ownership

The visible body keeps its square aspect ratio and turns to stand on the actual supporting rim: bottom 0°, top 180°, left 90°, right -90°. Its face turns with its body; world pointer/travel vectors are projected into that orientation. Moving and dragging look toward travel; working/thinking/sleepy/sad preserve their activity attention. Idle eyes smoothly follow a fresh nearby pointer sample when the pointer is in front of the facing/travel direction, then return to native curiosity, with subtle local eye movement. The body observes hover directly; the existing shell input surfaces and all four painted water rims supply passive pointer samples. Idle gaze/notice extends to 420 scaled pixels, and nearby clicks to 460 (previously 190). After 650–1,149 ms of nearby presence, Aqua may wave, inspect or startle/evade, with personality-dependent chance and a 12–25 second cooldown. Passive proximity never accumulates annoyance and does not interrupt chat, movement, active tasks, anger or a directed feature visit. One finite deadline cancels on leave, hide or motion/interaction disable. Four thin input strips follow the actual perimeter thickness and retain dialog/Overview precedence; they do not cover the interior desktop or observe unrelated application windows. No mouse samples are sent over IPC.

QML layers slow bob, sway and moving reflections with different periods. The resting apex has no permanent sideways sweep; state-driven tip displacement is capped below one pixel at the native size. Tap uses a squash/rebound; positive and urgent expression changes use a short bounce and ripple. Happy/excited reactions add a bounded shine burst. Eyes crossfade between glossy, smiling and closed contours. Animation/effects policy still comes from Appearance/Abyss; hidden or motion-disabled bodies stop animation groups. Software Qt or a failed shader has a procedural vector fallback. Shader readiness uses an actually presented frame, since Qt may reuse a QSB reflection cache while reporting Uncompiled.

The material uses an implicit 3D volume, water-like refraction, Fresnel reflection, thickness-dependent absorption/translucency, a saturated illuminated core and small surface-normal ripples. The richer transmitted light retains colored detail instead of clipping each channel into a gray fill. Quality traces a bounded internal reflection and separate red/blue exit rays for subtle dispersion, with three depth samples of soft liquid focusing. Exactly two flattened feet and two higher side hands share the optical rig. `AquaPose.js` projects their positions and front/back order using the same three-axis matrix as the liquid volume, orbiting bubbles and face. The body rotates in object space before ray intersection, refraction and light evaluation; falls no longer rotate/squash a flat torso item. Depth scales by `1/(scaleX*scaleY)`, retaining the authored volume during subtle deformation. The face uses Qt's [Matrix4x4 transform](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qml-qtquick-matrix4x4.html); its front normal hides the face when the character turns away. The vector fallback keeps the same anatomy. The floor mirrors the body/limbs/bubbles/face layer at a fixed contact plane, compresses it in perspective and fades it with distance. Its capture never includes desktop content or recursively includes itself. The old detached theme-surface attachment oval is removed.

## Shared Abyss water

Every registered `AbyssBodyHost` can be Aqua's habitat once its real content and painted geometry have settled. This includes Dock, Settings, both Sidebars, ordinary and styled popup/IPC hosts, OSD, notifications and utility surfaces. The scene reads `geometry.surface`, avoiding inset content/input rectangles. Moving bodies remain obstacles for new visits, while a Aqua already on their rim is carried along its painted front. When the rim has enough room, presentation remains visible; balance uses alternating arms and a wobble. If space runs out, recover to a clear rim. A failed balance may fall to the first clear lower support. Complete host clearance, the field's actual record capacity and modal/security ownership remain authoritative; a full-screen Settings body with no clear rim does not force Aqua over its content. All four orientations require complete tangent support and exclude rounded corners. Dock remains open through Aqua's visit, arrival and final dive using semantic placement, without binding its open decision back to the derived scene. The same complete-host input Region is reused in both the ordinary and Utility masks; modal dialog and Overview drag precedence remain. Actual Quickshell Region/policy bindings are checked across 32 activation cases, separately from physical compositor input acceptance.

Peek, rise, depart, land, tap and dive publish a contact point and outward normal. `AquaAbyssLink` uses the actual presentation clock for a small surface-tension neck and one finite ripple envelope: 1,450 ms normally, 5,800 ms for the slower sinking action. Sinking also draws a local swirling depression at the current contact. `AbyssField` applies these to its existing distance/material pass before normals, shadow, reflection and refraction. The same style supplies colors and specular light. Separate emergence rings are disabled on the connected production host. When global Abyss waves are enabled, the contact also enters the existing circular Popup/Sidebar wave solver at the parent screen edge. Local contact follows Companion motion/effects policy; disabling either cancels it immediately. The existing wave solver is also sampled at Aqua’s contact on its own revision event; a meaningful crest can trigger the same balance pose without another clock or field copy. At rest or after settling, all contact uniforms return to zero. There is no added texture, render pass, window, process, polling loop or continuous IPC stream. These source budgets and the frozen field-only GPU controls do not establish whole-session resource savings or full-character pixel parity. Qt's [ShaderEffect contract](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qml-qtquick-shadereffect.html) governs the premultiplied material colors; the shader package retains its original six [QSB targets](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qtshadertools-qsb.html).

`qs -p aquaAbyss.qml` opens the development water study using the actual field, body registry, host and coupling. `python3 scripts/test-aqua-abyss-water.py` checks owned-window contacts, supported walking, body-water retreat, finite settling, parent waves and policy/capacity hide. `--abyss --surface dock --peek --output /absolute/new.png` captures a 3D emergence; Settings, both Sidebars, styled popup, OSD, utility and the screen edge are selectable. `--abyss --water-proof --output /absolute/new.png` captures three frozen field-only controls with the character excluded. Preview content is a fixture, not a second production popup system.

## Blender motion and runtime cost

`assets/aqua/AquaMotion.blend` contains the editable liquid mesh, two feet, two hands, eight orbital bubbles and **29 actions**. All limbs parent to the 3D body. Backside/faceplant/icy falls use pitch/yaw/roll while retaining a round liquid volume; they no longer flatten the torso. The saved eye meshes have actual eye-open drivers. RiseJump reaches about 83 native pixels and Launch about 108, with preparation and landing; stuck variants first struggle, then push away from the water. Buttplant and FallVanish add distinct seated landing and fall-into-water sequences. Sink lasts 5,600 ms with stalls, panicked flailing and closed eyes; a first icy opening may cause a backside fall and retry elsewhere. Complete normal arcs still need clearance; tight openings use a submerged transition.

Rebuild with Blender’s Python engine using `scripts/aqua-author-motion.py`. The exported module retains every authored LINEAR F-curve key and Blender’s stored float32 timing/value precision. The saved scene was reopened: **1,332 exact keys**, 29 actions and exactly two feet/two hands. **174 actual evaluated Blender spatial poses** agree with the runtime matrix within `5.960464477539063e-08`; the volume determinant error is at most `1.1920928955078125e-07`. Curve evaluation uses a per-key arithmetic/input-rounding bound derived from stored float32 precision, rather than widening a fixed global tolerance. [Readback](../../aqua-visual/spatial-20261004/blender-readback.json) records the saved asset and curve hashes.

The desktop loads the **30,958-byte shared scalar module** and existing procedural renderer. It never loads the authoring mesh, runs Blender, decodes animation video or sends frames over IPC. One gait clock handles looping poses; finite transitions use presentation progress, jump/fall use travel progress, and short reactions use the existing finite gesture clock. Upright rendering retains its analytic front path. Tilted poses search the volume using 24/32/40 samples by quality tier and seven refinements; no new pass, texture, animation clock, window or process is introduced. Exit-ray, particle and floor-texture budgets remain. The floor capture/contact plane is removed while airborne. Hidden or motion-disabled bodies stop their visual clocks. These structural budgets and exact key retention are not measured CPU/GPU/RAM savings or whole-image lossless proof.

`AquaPresence` chooses each visit dynamically from Top/Bottom/Left/Right inner water rims and every settled registered Abyss body. An available surface has a 35% initial visit chance. One twelve-second deadline gives a still-open surface a 62% movement offer; hover/tasks defer it until released. Complete scaled host rectangles and swept paths avoid Bar modules and occupied surfaces. A bounded visibility graph can fly around obstacles and rejects an unavailable path.

Walking runs at **16 native pixels/second**, and Run at **32**, the previous walking speed, only when the entire route has the same oriented support. Fly stays at **105**. A short supported route may use the Blender Jump action and a 42-pixel scaled arc, including preparation and landing. Other travel uses Fly at 105 pixels/second, with gentle acceleration and a curved ascent where space permits. Complete conservative swept envelopes reject overhead obstacles and output bounds, including between keyframes; tight flight corridors keep their clear straight route and animated propulsion. Dragging holds native hover permission, tracks safe output-local positions and does not create an extra tap. A high release may fall with increasing speed to the first reachable actual upper rim or screen floor, or propel itself upwards. Passive hover does not freeze a fall or release flight; a new grab interrupts either. Landing restores the floor reflection and plays Land with the shared water ripple. Reduced motion cancels automatic travel. A normal visibility deadline finishes emergence and guarantees eight seconds after full reveal before hiding, fixing immediate appear/disappear. Intentional peek-only withdrawal and policy hide remain immediate. Normal hide flies toward the nearest reachable body-water rim or screen edge at 300 pixels/second, then dives, sinks or falls through that newly reached boundary. Unsupported float-up offers are reduced to 3%; most movement uses supported walk/run, with flight for transit. Policy/backend loss cancels motion and input immediately. Peek holds at a partial presentation that shows the eyes. About 28% of introductions retract without fully appearing, wait briefly and choose another opening. Repeated near clicks or four fast drag reversals can annoy Aqua: it turns angry, runs/flies away or takes a 20–35 second break. Other near clicks randomly cause Startle/evade or Delight. Break deadlines survive scene changes and never force a hidden actor to appear. Partial presentation never captures input. Geometry relocation re-arms emergence even when reveal's value has not changed.

### Curious feature visits

**Explore Abyss features** is enabled by default, as requested by the maintainer; Companion itself remains default-off for a new installation. Its English switch is in Companion → Behavior. A visit has an 18% offer chance on an existing native wander event and at least a 90-second cooldown between attempts. Busy user surfaces, active tasks, reduced motion, modal/security ownership and unqualified geometry prevent an offer. There is no new polling loop, model inference or frame IPC.

Aqua approaches an actual Bar feature or water rim, performs Press/Reach and borrows the **existing mature StyledPopup** for Calendar, Resources, Battery, Weather, Media, Quicknotes or Notifications. Utilities uses its existing generic host; either Sidebar uses its existing presentation. It does not autonomously open Settings, Dashboard or Dock. Dock and Settings remain valid habitats when the user opens them.

The owning Bar module registers its real popup. Aqua’s lease enters the same visibility/content/slot lifecycle as hover; a real pointer request revokes the lease and keeps that popup open. There is no duplicate popup content or native window. Return or departure can close only the lease still owned by Aqua. After Aqua has reached a feature, a gap beyond 140 scaled pixels closes it while preserving the departure animation. External departure cancels the old inspect/hold return deadline and has one bounded deadline of its own. Clicking/dragging Aqua or using its speech cloud interrupts and closes an unclaimed visit instead of leaving a sticky surface. Actual feature hover hands the existing surface to the user; visited Sidebars become transient hover surfaces, and Utilities uses its existing hover/focus idle close after the companion hold ends. Inspect/Press/Wave varies by feature. Autonomous visits retain the current application’s keyboard focus. Curiosity changes no file, setting, playback or notification data.

## Companion settings

The Rendering section includes a bounded 0..35% Translucency control, initially 16%. It lowers the liquid's opacity slightly while Fresnel edges and bright reflection catches recover opacity; the glossy eyes keep their independent dense material. It composites the actual background through the body without capturing or distorting desktop pixels. The percentage describes the material control, not uniform whole-character transparency.

The English **Settings > Abyss > Companion** page has Overview, Behavior, Rendering and **AI** sections. Its live preview reuses `WaterDropletBody`; it starts no second daemon. Right-clicking an interactive Aqua opens this page through the existing connected Settings surface. Size lives in Rendering. Fixed display, edge and along-edge controls are removed; old persisted values remain schema-compatible but are ignored by normalized placement. Pointer response, an additional fullscreen hide preference, animation and effects controls persist through the existing typed config. Reset affects Companion only.

Personality choices are Calm, Balanced and Energetic. They change local motion amplitude plus native idle cadence, reaction energy/targets and positive expressions. Appearance frequency is Always visible, Every minute, Every 3 minutes or Every 10 minutes. Visits last twenty seconds; interaction extends them, hovering holds them, and an ongoing task holds them until completion. A shell hide cancels both the visibility permission and all automatic deadlines. Defaults preserve the previous always-visible Balanced behavior when enabled. Optional conversation uses the bounded semantic intent adapter described below. Current AI planning and model/runtime qualification live only in the [canonical Local AI plan](../../../to-do/cloud-bot/AQUA_LOCAL_AI.md).

## Speech and check-in presentation

Talk clouds use cute English messages and a thin themed border, with no title, description, hint or extra text action labels. The two hover actions are small, fully curved cloud shapes with accent borders, placed on an inward-facing orbit around the Companion. Their placement adapts to Top/Bottom/Left/Right edges and corners; each cloud supplies its own input footprint rather than capturing the space between them. Mood asks one question and shows one five-choice row; after a successful choice, Energy asks its separate question and shows its row. The values match the Obsidian Journal view: Awful/Low/Okay/Good/Great and Drained/Low/Mid/High/Peak. There is no prompt input during either question. Explicit choices save through the existing Todo daily-journal location; no unanswered value is invented. The persistence/provider contracts and AI qualification are recorded only in the [canonical Local AI plan](../../../to-do/cloud-bot/AQUA_LOCAL_AI.md).

**Super + Alt + Comma** toggles the explicit chat editor and focuses its input. Enter submits; Escape cancels/dismisses. The editor has no extra send/close/done/source buttons, and closing preserves its draft. Passive messages/check-ins never take keyboard focus. Aqua pauses travel while a question or explicit conversation is active, and clicks inside the cloud cannot count as a nearby disturbance. [Actual owned-item captures](../../aqua-visual/spatial-20261004/README.md) show both questions and the explicit editor separately.
